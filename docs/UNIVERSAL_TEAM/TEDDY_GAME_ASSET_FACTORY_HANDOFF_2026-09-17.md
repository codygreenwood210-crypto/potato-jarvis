## Story canon lock

The complete story supplied by the user on 2026-09-17 controls asset production.

### Immutable canon rules

1. Teddy is **ONE person**.
2. Teddy is not split into a good personality and an evil personality.
3. Nightmare magic is **NOT evil**. It represents fear, anger, grief, pain, instinct, uncertainty, chaos and difficult memories; it is necessary alongside Heartland magic.
4. Heartland magic is not simply moral goodness; it represents hope, courage, imagination, love, creation, protection and connection.
5. Teddy's golden and violet powers belong to the same Teddy.
6. The red stitched heart is a living bridge binding Heartland and Nightmare magic.
7. There is **NO separate evil Shadow Teddy** in the main story.
8. The Unraveler represents forced separation/control and the attempt to remove painful emotions.
9. Teddy represents integration and directed choice: feeling everything without allowing one feeling to decide everything.
10. Heartland and Nightmare are both necessary.
11. The user has now supplied the ending, so ending-state production art may be created consistently with that story. The post-credits sealed-door mystery remains intentionally unresolved.

## Story progression summary

- Chapter 1 — Halloween festival in Moonstitch Hollow; Teddy and Ghost Child attend the Heart Lantern ceremony; Teddy's stitched heart resonates; the Heart Lantern explodes; Heart Shards scatter; The Unraveler's voice is heard.
- Chapter 2 — Teddy awakens after the disaster, helps villagers and begins the Heart Shard quest.
- Chapter 3 — Whispering Forest; Pumpkin King is overwhelmed rather than evil; Teddy combines golden and violet magic to stabilise him and gains the first Heart Shard.
- Chapter 4 — Forgotten Graveyard; Headless Knight challenges Teddy; the story explicitly establishes that darkness is not automatically corruption; second Heart Shard recovered.
- Chapter 5 — Broken Castle; Teddy learns he was created from the remaining power of the ancient Heartland and Nightmare Guardians; Chaos Surge teaches integration rather than suppression.
- Chapter 6 — Bone Caverns; Bone Dragon tests Teddy; The Unraveler is revealed as the former Keeper who tried to remove painful emotions and created imbalance.
- Chapter 7 — Collision of Worlds; Moonstitch overlaps the Nightmare Realm; allies return; Ghost Child is overwhelmed and rescued without being framed as evil.
- Chapter 8 — The Last Stitch; The Unraveler tries to sever Teddy's living bridge; Teddy integrates both magics; Heart Shards rebuild the Heart Lantern; The Unraveler is defeated.
- Chapter 9 — Teddy spares the ancient Keeper beneath The Unraveler and restores the Lantern without erasing responsibility or grief.
- Epilogue — Moonstitch is rebuilt and changed by the journey; one year later Teddy answers “Both” when Ghost Child asks which side he really is.
- Post-credits — an ancient sealed doorway reacts to a third unidentified magical thread and a voice whispers “Keeper…?”

## Canonical Teddy design

The Jester Teddy remains vertically split between golden/yellow Heartland magic and violet Nightmare magic, with a central stitched seam, golden eye, glowing violet eye, purple-side horn, tongue, red stitched heart patch, yellow leg, purple leg, red/purple/gold jester hat and bells, jester collar, patch/diamond motifs, and the canonical double-headed axe/halberd.

Directional sprites must preserve anatomical continuity. **Do not simply mirror Teddy if mirroring swaps which anatomical side is golden versus purple.**

## Canon rejection / legacy-name rule

Legacy assets named `dark_teddy`, `corrupted_teddy`, `shadow_teddy` or `giant_dark_teddy` are **REJECTED for the main narrative role**. They may only be renamed unrelated enemies, used as explicitly non-canon challenge content, used as dream/memory imagery that is clearly not another Teddy personality, or excluded.

Default main-story treatment: exclude them.
