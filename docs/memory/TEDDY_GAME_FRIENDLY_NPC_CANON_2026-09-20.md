## Blair — Village Gardener
Blair is the canonical female Village Gardener.

Visual direction:
- warm brown teddy-bear villager
- green floral headscarf/bandana
- cream gardening blouse
- green-and-cream apron
- sturdy brown gardening boots
- seed/tool satchel
- watering can
- white/yellow flowers and leafy accents

Personality:
- kind
- patient
- practical
- observant
- quietly brave
- gently humorous

Story function:
Blair represents care that creates visible change. She helps the village grow and recover, notices unusual changes in plants, and supports the core Teddy Game theme that Teddy's golden and purple/Nightmare halves are inseparable parts of one whole. She must never treat the purple half as evil or separate.

Gameplay function:
- gardening and restoration quests
- seeds, herbs, and crafting materials
- village beautification
- environmental secrets and shortcuts
- optional persistent hub upgrades

Dialogue motif:
"Little things grow brighter days."

## Gardener correction
Blair replaces the older provisional gardener Sprout. Sprout is superseded as the gardener and remains only a historical/provisional concept unless the creator explicitly restores that character in another role.
