## Promotion discipline

Promote only from representative benchmark evidence. Include edge cases, adversarial cases, ambiguity, stale-information traps, tool failures, and recovery where relevant. Separate self-evaluation from independent verification.

## Demotion discipline

Downgrade stale or contradicted mastery claims. Revalidate after meaningful model, tool, API, standard, or domain changes.

**Standing principle:** NO TITLE-BASED EXPERTISE. EARN IT. PROVE IT. KEEP IT CURRENT.
